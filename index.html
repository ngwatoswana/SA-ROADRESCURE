About RoadRescue SA

RoadRescue SA is an emergency response platform designed to connect drivers with the right assistance during accidents, vehicle breakdowns, and roadside emergencies.

Our system allows users to request help, share their location, and receive support from mechanics, hospitals, police, and towing services through one connected platform.
